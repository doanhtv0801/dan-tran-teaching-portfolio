Teaching philosophy

Learn by building

Students learn technology most effectively by creating something themselves — a personal website, a small game, a data visualisation, an AI application, an automation tool, a digital portfolio. Every project builds technical knowledge and problem-solving in the same motion.

Start with the problem

Not: "Today we are learning loops."
Instead: "Here's a problem. How do we make a computer solve it?"

From there, students discover for themselves why they need variables, conditions, loops, functions and algorithms.

Encourage curiosity

Technology changes fast, so memorising one language matters less than learning how to ask questions, investigate, experiment, fail, improve and build.

Responsible use of AI

Rather than simply preventing students from using AI, schools should teach them to use it responsibly — what it can and can't do, how to verify its output, and the ethics, privacy, bias and academic-integrity questions that come with it.
